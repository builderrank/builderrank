## Pricing / Plan Parameters Observed

Public pricing describes:

- Essential: tracking on ChatGPT and Gemini.
- Essential: 3 competitors per hotel.
- Essential: 3 traveler personas.
- Essential: OTA vs direct booking link analysis.
- Pro: all platforms and models.
- Pro: advanced competitive benchmarking and market analysis.
- Pro: aggregated reporting across hotels.
- Enterprise: custom models, white-label reporting, SSO, API access, role-based permissions.
- Website builder add-on with AI-optimized content structure, schema markup, FAQ integration, mobile-first performance, and structured data.

## Research Methodology Observed

The public 2026 AI Hotel Landscape report describes:

- 2,500 unique prompts.
- 25 cities.
- 8 traveler personas.
- 9 hotel types.
- 3, 4, and 5 star tiers where applicable.
- Multiple AI models including ChatGPT/GPT variants, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok.
- Link destination analysis.
- Source/citation analysis.
- OTA and user-generated-content source analysis.

## Builder Rank Translation

For Builder Rank, the equivalent parameter set should be:

- Job type instead of hotel type.
- Service market instead of travel destination.
- Homeowner/investor/emergency/insurance personas instead of traveler personas.
- Direct website vs Google Business Profile/directory/review/social links instead of direct hotel vs OTA links.
- Contractor competitors instead of hotel competitors.
- Trust, specialty, project proof, availability, license, warranty, value, and communication attributes instead of hotel amenities/perception attributes.
