# Builder Rank Marketing Engine Demo Guide

Use this as the plain-English talk track for early users. The dashboard is organized into three categories: AI visibility, website tracking, and marketing execution.

## Global Summary Strip

The top strip gives the marketing team a quick health check:

- Site Signal: whether Builder Rank is receiving website activity from the installed tracking snippet.
- Visibility Score: a blended score for how often AI tools can find and recommend the business.
- AI Referrals: sessions that appear to come from, or be assisted by, AI search and assistant behavior.
- AI Lead Rate: how often AI-assisted sessions turn into calls, forms, quote clicks, or other lead actions.
- Quote Events: tracked estimate, call, form, phone, and email actions.
- Punch List: the number of recommended marketing fixes still waiting to be started or approved.

## Visibility Blueprint

This page answers: "Are AI tools recommending us for the jobs we want?"

- Mention Rate shows how often the business appears in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude answers for service and market prompts.
- Avg. Position shows where the business tends to rank when it is mentioned.
- Mention Rate by Job-Type Attribute shows which profit-center terms are strong or weak, such as walk-in showers, luxury bathrooms, warranty clarity, emergency HVAC, roofing, or restoration.

Why it matters: contractors can see whether AI understands their best services, not just whether the website gets traffic.

## Competition

This page answers: "Who is AI recommending instead of us?"

- Overall Mention Rank compares the customer against local competitors in the selected market and service category.
- Rankings show mention rate and platform performance for each competitor.
- Avg. position shows where each business appears when it gets recommended.

Why it matters: a customer can see whether competitors are winning because of stronger content, reviews, source coverage, or clearer service pages.

## Sources

This page answers: "Where is AI getting its information about us?"

- Sources Share shows how much AI and website attribution is coming from the direct website, directories, Google Business Profile, social/user-generated sources, and local media.
- All Sources lists specific domains such as the customer website, Google Maps, Houzz, Angi, BBB, Reddit, or YouTube.
- Lead Signal shows whether a source is also tied to real website sessions or leads.

Why it matters: AI visibility is not only about the website. It is also shaped by trusted third-party pages and review profiles.

## Citations

This page answers: "Which prompts mention us, cite us, or miss us?"

- Prompt Citations shows each monitored homeowner prompt.
- Mentioned shows yes, partial, or no.
- Rank shows where the customer appeared in the answer.
- Linked Source shows whether AI used the website, GBP, or a third-party source.
- Recommended Fix tells the marketing team what to improve next.

Why it matters: this turns AI search from a mystery into a list of specific prompts and fixes.

## Site Signal

This page answers: "Is Builder Rank connected to the customer website?"

- Connect this website shows the Site Signal snippet and the customer's Site ID.
- Site Signal status confirms whether events are coming in.
- Tracked Sessions, AI Referrals, AI Lead Rate, and Quote Events show live website activity after install.
- Tracked Website Events lists page views, phone clicks, quote clicks, form submits, and email clicks by page and source.

Why it matters: this is how the dashboard becomes real customer reporting instead of a static report.

## Leads & Events

This page answers: "Which AI and marketing sources are turning into real leads?"

- Top AI Landing Page identifies the strongest page receiving AI-assisted traffic.
- Top CTA / Form shows which button or form is generating the most intent.
- Highest Intent Source identifies the source producing the strongest lead behavior.
- Next Best Moves prioritizes actions based on live traffic, conversion gaps, and AI visibility data.
- Lead Events by Source breaks down sessions, calls, forms, quote clicks, top intent, and lead rate.
- Job Pipeline by Intent groups activity by service category.
- Top CTAs & Forms shows which calls, forms, and quote buttons are actually being used.

Why it matters: a marketing team can stop guessing and see which pages, services, and sources create pipeline.

## Landing Pages

This page answers: "Which service pages are working, and which ones need help?"

- Landing Pages lists key service pages.
- AI Sessions shows AI-assisted traffic.
- Phone Clicks and Forms show conversion actions.
- Conversion shows how well traffic turns into leads.
- Top Intent tells which job type the page is attracting.
- Content Gap recommends what the page needs, such as schema, FAQs, proof, or a stronger CTA.

Why it matters: it connects page-level performance to the actual services the contractor wants to sell.

## Punch List

This page answers: "What should Builder Rank change next?"

- Builder Rank Punch List lists recommended website, content, schema, GBP, and review tasks.
- Each task includes expected impact and a plain-English action.
- Live Change Log records completed changes after they are applied.

Why it matters: this is the bridge from reporting to execution. The customer can approve work instead of just reading a dashboard.

## Reviews & GBP

This page answers: "What local trust signals need to improve?"

- Reviews & Google Business Profile Tasks lists review prompts, GBP service updates, GBP posts, and profile improvements.
- Service ties each task to a profit center.
- Channel identifies whether the task belongs to GBP, reviews, posts, or local citations.
- Impact helps prioritize the work.

Why it matters: AI tools rely heavily on local trust signals, not only website copy.

## Reports

This page answers: "What has been inspected and saved for this customer?"

- Reports stores saved AI inspections, website tracking checks, and report history.
- Run new inspection lets the customer start another report.
- Export CSV gives the marketing team portable reporting data.

Why it matters: customers can come back, see prior results, and understand progress over time.
