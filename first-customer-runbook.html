Beta onboarding

First Customer Runbook

Use this checklist to manually onboard a GC or service business into the Builder Rank Marketing Engine without waiting for a full self-serve flow.

Download doc Open admin beta
1

Collect Intake

Capture business name, website, market, trade, contact info, services, competitors, install method, and the customer’s main goal.

2

Clear Launch Readiness

Open `/admin-beta` and confirm required launch-readiness blockers are zero before customer setup.

3

Bootstrap Workspace

Use `/admin-beta` to create the Site Signal ID, job types, competitors, starter prompts, and first Punch List tasks.

4

Install Site Signal

Add the tracking snippet or WordPress plugin to the customer’s production website and avoid logged-in admin traffic.

5

Run QA

Send one page-view test and one lead-event test. Confirm events are stored and the tracked domain matches the workspace domain.

6

Import AI Checks

Run service prompts in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude. Save mentions, rank, answer summaries, cited sources, and recommended fixes.

7

Connect Account

Have the customer sign in, connect their Site Signal ID, and confirm live data and report history appear in their dashboard.

8

Review Dashboard

Walk through Visibility, Competition, Sources, Site Signal, Leads, Landing Pages, and Punch List. Pick the first tasks to execute.

9

Follow Up

Send the dashboard link, top Punch List tasks, weekly check cadence, and record feedback in HubSpot.

Go / no-go

Customer review should only happen when the workspace is ready.

  • Launch readiness required blockers are zero.
  • Workspace and Site Signal ID created.
  • Snippet or WordPress plugin installed.
  • Page-view and lead-event QA stored.
  • Domain QA matches the customer website.
  • Competitors, AI checks, and Punch List are seeded.
  • Customer account is connected and scoped to the correct workspace.
