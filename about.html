About Builder Rank

We help contractors see whether AI can understand and recommend them.

Builder Rank grades a contractor website for the signals AI assistants need before they can confidently connect a homeowner to a local business: entity trust, crawlability, service clarity, local proof, schema, reviews, and answer-ready content.

What we check

Readable local proof

Reports inspect service areas, contact details, project evidence, reviews, technical crawlability, and schema that helps models understand the contractor as a real local entity.

Who it is for

GCs and service businesses

Builder Rank is built for contractors who want to know how their website looks to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and the next generation of AI search tools.

What customers get

A report and prioritized fixes

Each paid report includes model-specific analysis, an AI Health Score, recommended fixes, PDF delivery, JSON export, and saved account history.

Product roadmap

From audit to autopilot

The first Builder Rank release is a paid report card. The roadmap is to turn those audits into account history, launch operations, and eventually ongoing visibility tracking by market.
